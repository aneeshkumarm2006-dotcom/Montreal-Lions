Meet the Lions

The lineup.

Four players, four signature tips. Open a plate to learn how each Lion plays the game.

Stevie Petropouleas

“I’ve learned that every mistake is a lesson: learn, adapt, and become a better player.”

Communicate with your partner.

Why it matters
Communication ensures coordination, prevents confusion and strengthens teamwork.
How I do it
“I call shots and let my partner know when I’m moving or covering a specific area.”

Ernesto Fajardo

“I believe the true victory is found in every rally, not just the scoreboard.”

Master the dink shot.

Why it matters
The dink controls the game, forces movement and creates attacking chances in the kitchen.
How I do it
“I keep my dinks low and place them strategically to force my opponent to move and make mistakes.”

Anne-Sophie Courteau

“For me, pickleball isn’t just about skills; it’s about the heart I put into every point.”

Focus on a solid serve.

Why it matters
A solid serve sets the tone and gives you control.
How I do it
“I keep my serve consistent and deep, mixing in spin and targeting different areas to keep my opponent guessing.”

Louis-Charles Amyot

“In pickleball, as in life, consistency and perseverance lead to greatness.”

Stay in the ready position.

Why it matters
Quick reactions, balance and shot anticipation.
How I do it
“I stay balanced with my paddle in front of me, ready to move quickly in any direction.”

Jay Devilliers

Before that

Celebrated professional pickleball player and one of the proud owners of the Montreal Lions. A dominant force in pro pickleball, his dynamic athleticism and precision shot-making have earned him success across all three divisions.

The French-born former soccer and tennis player switched to pickleball in 2019 and rose quickly to elite status. Off the court he’s a global ambassador for the sport, growing it internationally and inspiring the next generation.
